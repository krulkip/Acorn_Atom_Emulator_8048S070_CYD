ATOM 6502 CYD V073a
====================

Compile-only correction to V073.

V073 added fast cassette hooks in fakemem.c that access:
  *fake6502_a
  *fake6502_status

Those register pointers already exist in the CPU core, but fakemem.c did not
declare them as extern symbols.

V073a adds:
  extern uint8_t *fake6502_a;
  extern uint8_t *fake6502_status;

No cassette, CPU, keyboard, video, sound or GAMEKEYS behaviour is changed.
