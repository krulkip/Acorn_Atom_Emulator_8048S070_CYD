ATOM 6502 CYD V075a
====================

Compile-only correction to V075.

V075 reads clockticks6502 from bitboard_6502.c for the CPU speed diagnostic,
but that global is defined in fake6502.c and was not declared in this
translation unit.

V075a adds:
  extern uint32_t clockticks6502;

No timing or emulator behavior has been changed.
