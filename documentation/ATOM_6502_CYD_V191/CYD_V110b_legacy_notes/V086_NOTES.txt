ATOM 6502 CYD V086
===================

Built directly from V085 / stable V079a audio path.

Purpose
-------
Determine what GPIO26 physically does immediately around an Invaders shot.

V085 proved that the firing routine:
  $356C LDA $B002
  $356F EOR #$04
  $3571 STA $B002
causes a real GPIO26 edge.

V086 now records every physical GPIO26 edge in a rolling history using
micros() wall-clock timestamps.

For each successful FIRE write it reports:
  50 ms BEFORE the shot
  through
  100 ms AFTER the shot

Example:
  [FIRE-WINDOW]       +12 us PC=$3574 GPIO26 1->0 <FIRE>
  [FIRE-WINDOW]     +8450 us PC=$xxxx GPIO26 0->1
  ...

This answers whether another PC2/GPIO transition immediately follows the
single firing edge and effectively turns it into a very short pulse.

Test
----
Run Invaders with GAMEKEYS ON.
Press SPACE once, preferably with a pause before the next SPACE.
Wait ~0.2 seconds for the FIRE-WINDOW report.
Send the complete FIRE-WINDOW block.

No sound behavior is changed.
