ATOM 6502 CYD V102a
====================

Based on V102. Keyboard/emulator behaviour is unchanged.

Screenshot colour correction
----------------------------
V102 successfully saved 320x240 24-bit BMP screenshots, but on the tested
JC2432W328/ST7789 CYD the TFT readback returned the displayed green phosphor in
the wrong RGB byte position, producing blue BMP pixels.

V102a does not hard-code a green/blue special case. Immediately after TFT init,
before the visible boot screen is drawn, it writes one red, green and blue test
pixel, reads each back with readRectRGB(), restores those pixels to black, and
derives the TFT readback channel permutation. Screenshot conversion then uses
that calibrated channel mapping when writing BMP BGR bytes.

Calibration details are reported only on Serial. No screenshot status or
calibration text is drawn on the CYD.

The screenshot path remains:
  /ATOM/SCREENSHOTS/ATOM0001.BMP
  /ATOM/SCREENSHOTS/ATOM0002.BMP
  ...

WDT handling remains as in V102: capture is one scanline at a time with yield()
after every row.
