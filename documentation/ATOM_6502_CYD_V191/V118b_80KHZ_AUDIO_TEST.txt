V120c audio sampling test

Based directly on V120.

Only intended functional change:
- I2S/audio sample rate: 40 kHz -> 80 kHz

Everything else from V120 remains unchanged:
- raw PC2 edge timing
- same AC-coupling/high-pass model
- no V120a low-pass changes
- no synthetic decay
- AtoMMC/filesystem/ROM banking/keyboard unchanged

Purpose:
- preserve the already-correct alien/turret/mothership destruction sounds
- recover the missing fire sound
- reduce the aliasing/tik-tok character of the mothership flying sound

At 80 kHz, one sample is 12.5 us instead of 25 us.
