V123a serial options

At the top of the .ino:

#define SERIAL_SHOW_CPU_DIAGNOSTIC       0
#define SERIAL_SHOW_RX_MATRIX_DIAGNOSTIC 0

Set either to 1 if that diagnostic is wanted.

These switches do NOT suppress normal boot/status serial messages.
They also do NOT affect the Atom output mirror controlled by $BFFE.

Atom output mirror:
  ?#BFFE=1   ON
  ?#BFFE=0   OFF

The included ASBK EXEC source is the fixed-geometry Debug5 source with:
  1095?#BFFE=1
so its screen output is mirrored to serial during ASBK assembly/testing.

PSRAM must remain enabled for the 8048S070 ESP32-S3 build.
