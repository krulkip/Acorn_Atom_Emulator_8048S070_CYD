V152 BBC BASIC / ASBK vector diagnostic

Based directly on V151.

Behaviour is intentionally unchanged from V151:
- ATBASIC3 remains at $4000-$7FFF.
- On first BBC entry WRCVEC is restored to $FE52 and RDCVEC to $FE94.
- COMVEC and all filing vectors remain untouched.

New diagnostic only:
- Before restoring the character vectors, prints all live vectors $0200-$021B.
- Prints ASBK workspace/saved-vector area $9F00-$9F1B.
- Highlights live WRCVEC/RDCVEC/COMVEC and $9F08/$9F0A.

No 6522, AtoMMC, ASBK init, keyboard, SD, audio, or memory-map changes.
