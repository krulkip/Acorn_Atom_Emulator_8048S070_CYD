V178 - ASBK-S V24 only in Atom mode

Baseline: proven V177 BBC boot.

- Ordinary cold Atom boot and ordinary BREAK restore the proven V141/V139
  ASBK-S V24 initialization at the first post-reset OSRDCH ($FE94).
- After BREAK, V24 is reloaded into RAM $1000-$1660 before LL97 at $1005.
- SHIFT+BREAK does NOT reload or initialize ASBK-S. The real Atom reset clears
  the ASBK RAM workspace, natural vectors are retained, and the proven V177
  one-character-per-OSRDCH LINK #4000 path enters BBC BASIC.
- V177 BBC screen clear/home on entry to $4000-$7FFF is unchanged.
- ASBK-S V24 is unchanged: static AtoMMC V3.02E compatibility vectors,
  including COMVEC $E0DE and the proven filing vectors.
