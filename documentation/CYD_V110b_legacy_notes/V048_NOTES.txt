V048 modifier lifetime correction

Baseline: V047.

Observed on CYD:
  typed: 10 PRINT "HELLO"'
  shown: 10 PRINT 2hello27

Root cause found in actual V047 source:
- At ROM PC FEB1 the queued physical key was committed and V047 immediately
  cleared kbd_shift/kbd_ctrl.
- The Atom ROM does not test SHIFT/CTRL until later in its decode path
  (BIT $B001 at FDD8/FDE2).
- Therefore shifted punctuation lost SHIFT before decoding: " became 2,
  apostrophe became 7.

V048 change:
- At FEB1 consume/release only the matrix key row/column.
- Preserve SHIFT/CTRL through the ROM decode.
- Clear SHIFT/CTRL at FDE9, after the ROM modifier tests have completed.

Everything else from V047 is unchanged.
