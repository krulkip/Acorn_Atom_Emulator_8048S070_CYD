ATOM 6502 CYD V076
===================

Baseline
--------
Built directly from V075a / V074 feature set.

Measured before V076
--------------------
Idle at >        : ~0.403 MHz
10 GOTO 10       : ~0.56 MHz
INVADERS gameplay: ~0.53 MHz

Cause
-----
The previous timing method delayed once every 256 instructions. Since 6502
instructions take different cycle counts, the resulting MHz depended strongly
on workload.

V076 governor
-------------
Normal execution is governed by actual clockticks6502:

  1,000,000 emulated 6502 cycles = 1.000 real second

Equivalently:
  1 emulated cycle = 1 real microsecond

The CPU runs freely while behind schedule. If it gets ahead, V076 yields and
then performs a very short fine wait to align with the target cycle timeline.

The governor periodically rebases after about one million cycles to avoid
long-term arithmetic drift.

Fast cassette
-------------
During the V073+ fast virtual-cassette path, the 1 MHz governor is intentionally
disabled. This preserves sub-second LOAD speed. When entering or leaving tape
turbo the governor reference is rebased so normal execution does not try to
catch up or compensate.

BREAK
-----
~BREAK also rebases both governor and diagnostic timing after reset.
