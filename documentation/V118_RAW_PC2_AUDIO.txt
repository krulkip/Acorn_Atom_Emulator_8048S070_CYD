V120c raw-PC2 audio test

Based on stable V117 AtoMMC/filesystem/ROM-bank code.

Audio change only:
- removes the synthetic kick + 8-step exponential decay
- removes all per-PC2-edge delayMicroseconds() calls
- timestamps genuine PC2 transitions in 6502 cycles
- reconstructs the original 0/1 waveform at 40 kHz I2S
- applies only a one-pole 63/64 AC-coupling high-pass filter

Purpose:
Preserve the distinct original Invaders waveforms (lower alien, upper alien,
mothership, fire, march and cannon/player-death) instead of making every edge
trigger the same synthetic transient.
